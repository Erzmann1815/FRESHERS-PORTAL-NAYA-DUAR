05 / 05 · Community & Senior Circle

Need Guidance? Ask a Senior!

Got questions about events, team formations, lab kits, or campus life? We've got your back.

Q: Can 1st-year freshers participate in multiple events?

The guide lists events from November 5 through November 9, 2026. Check each event's date and start time before registering; some events overlap.

Q: What if I don't have a team yet?

Registration requirements differ by event. HackShield and Circuit Sprint are individual events; team events ask for team or player details in their registration instructions.

Q: Do we get hardware kits for robotics & IoT?

The guide does not list provided equipment. Circuit Sprint asks participants to choose a hardware kit configuration; check with its organizers for kit availability.

Q: How do entry fees work for freshers?

Circuit Sprint, Sonic Glow, ArtCafe, and RunFor5K are marked free in the guide. Paid entry ranges from ₹150 per participant to ₹500 per team or group; see each event for its fee basis.
